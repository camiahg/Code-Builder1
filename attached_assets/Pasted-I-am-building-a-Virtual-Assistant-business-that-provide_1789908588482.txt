I am building a Virtual Assistant business that provides affordable, reliable remote support to people who are overwhelmed with everyday administrative and customer-service tasks. My goal is to help business owners and entrepreneurs get some of their time back so they can focus on running and growing their business.
I don't want to limit myself to one type of customer. I want to be able to work with solo entrepreneurs, online sellers, small businesses, and other customers who need help with administrative work, customer communication, organization, and other tasks that can be handled remotely.
What I Offer
My starting services will focus on work I can confidently provide while continuing to build my digital skills.
These services can include:
Inbox and email organization
Customer service and follow-up
Basic data entry
Online research
Document organization
File organization
Basic administrative support
Scheduling/calendar assistance
Other simple remote administrative tasks
AI-Assisted Administrative Support (Developing)
As I complete my AI and Digital Skills training, I plan to gradually incorporate AI tools into the services I provide. I am not offering AI training to customers right now. Instead, I want to learn how to use AI responsibly as a tool that can help me work more efficiently, such as assisting with research, organization, drafting, and repetitive administrative tasks. I will review AI-assisted work before it is given to a customer.
This allows my services to grow as my skills grow instead of me advertising services I am not ready to provide yet.
My Pricing
My starting baseline is $20 per hour.
For hourly work, I can track time in 15-minute increments:
15 minutes = $5
30 minutes = $10
45 minutes = $15
1 hour = $20
I will also offer flat-rate services starting at $25 for smaller, clearly defined tasks. Larger projects will generally start around $50+, depending on the amount and complexity of the work.
I can also offer packages for customers who need ongoing support. For example:
Basic: 10 hours/month — $175
Standard: 25 hours/month — $450
Premium: 50 hours/month — $850
Custom: Price based on the customer's needs
These package prices give customers a discount compared with my regular $20 hourly rate while giving me more predictable recurring work.
If a customer's project becomes larger than what we originally agreed on, I will stop before doing the additional work, explain what changed, give them the new price, and get their approval before continuing. This protects both the customer and my business from surprise charges.
Why Customers Would Choose Me
I know there are a lot of Virtual Assistants competing online, including people who charge much less than I do. Some international freelance VAs may charge only a few dollars per hour, while U.S.-based VA rates are generally higher.
I don't want my business to compete by simply being the cheapest option.
I want to compete through reliability, communication, flexibility, organization, and personalized service. My background in customer service and fast-paced work environments has taught me how important it is to communicate, follow instructions, stay organized, solve problems, and get things done.
I am also continuing to develop my digital and AI skills. That means my business has room to grow instead of staying limited to basic administrative work.
My Business Goal
My immediate goal is to start small, gain real customers, learn from each project, and build a strong reputation.

I want to start at $20/hour because I am entering the freelance market and want my pricing to match where I am now. As I gain experience, testimonials, stronger digital skills, and more experience using AI tools, I can increase my rates and expand my services.
My long-term goal is to build a professional remote-support business that combines human customer service, administrative support, digital skills, and eventually AI-assisted services.
I don't expect to know everything on day one. I plan to learn as I build, improve as I gain experience, and let the business grow alongside my customers and my skills.
